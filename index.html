What they need

  1. A top‑6 forward who scores goals.A shooter who gets to the net. The team has playmakers; it lacks someone who finishes.
  2. A defenseman who can run the power play.Moving the puck and shooting from the point would help the 0‑for‑16 power play and the defensemen's missing points.
  3. Better discipline.A coaching fix, not a new player. Fewer penalties means less time killing them and more time with the puck.
  4. A stronger backup goalie.Lower priority. DiVecchio can carry the job.
